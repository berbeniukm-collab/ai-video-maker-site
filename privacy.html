Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Video Maker handles information when users create videos or connect third-party services such as social media platforms.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features used, the application may process:

  • Video, audio, images, subtitles and other media provided or generated by the user.
  • Project settings such as language, voice, subtitle style and render options.
  • Basic account authorization data received from connected third-party services.
  • Access and refresh tokens required to maintain an authorized connection to a third-party service.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide requested features, such as creating or editing videos, generating subtitles or voiceovers, and publishing a completed video to an account authorized by the user.

4. Social Media Authorization

When the user connects a social media account, authorization is handled through the platform's official authentication flow. The application does not ask the user to provide their social media password directly.

5. Publishing

The application does not publish content to a connected social media account unless the user has authorized the connection and explicitly requested or enabled publishing.

6. Data Storage

Project files and generated media may be stored locally on the user's device. Authorization tokens may also be stored locally so the application can remain connected to an approved third-party account. Users should protect access to their device and application files.

7. Sharing

Information is not sold to advertisers. Data may be sent to third-party services only when necessary to perform a feature requested by the user, such as AI generation, voice synthesis, media processing, or publishing to an authorized social media account.

8. Data Deletion

Users can remove locally stored project files and authorization credentials from their device. Users may also revoke third-party account access through the connected platform's account or security settings.

9. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect credentials and local files. No system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or platform rules.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as features or integrations change. The current version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the official communication channel associated with AI Video Maker.

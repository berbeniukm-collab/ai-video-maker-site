Creator tool

Create, edit and prepare short-form videos.

AI Video Maker is a desktop creator tool that helps users generate, edit, translate, subtitle, render and prepare short-form videos for publishing.

View Privacy Policy

What the app does

  • Generates and edits video projects.
  • Creates subtitles and translated versions.
  • Creates voiceovers using user-selected settings.
  • Can prepare a completed video for publishing to the user's own social media account after explicit authorization.

User control

The application does not publish content to a social media account unless the user has authorized the connection and explicitly requested or enabled publishing.
